## Resistor colour code
Four-band: band 1 = first digit, band 2 = second digit, band 3 = multiplier, band 4 = tolerance.

| Colour | Digit | Multiplier | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | 0 | $\times 1$ | — |
| Brown | 1 | $\times 10$ | ±1 % |
| Red | 2 | $\times 100$ | ±2 % |
| Orange | 3 | $\times 1\text{k}$ | — |
| Yellow | 4 | $\times 10\text{k}$ | — |
| Green | 5 | $\times 100\text{k}$ | ±0.5 % |
| Blue | 6 | $\times 1\text{M}$ | ±0.25 % |
| Violet | 7 | $\times 10\text{M}$ | ±0.1 % |
| Grey | 8 | — | ±0.05 % |
| White | 9 | — | — |
| Gold | — | $\times 0.1$ | ±5 % |
| Silver | — | $\times 0.01$ | ±10 % |
| (none) | — | — | ±20 % |

Mnemonic: **B B ROY of Great Britain had a Very Good Wife** — Black Brown Red Orange Yellow
Green Blue Violet Grey White (0…9).

Examples:
- Brown–Black–Red–Gold $= 10 \times 100 = 1000\ \Omega = 1\ \text{k}\Omega \pm 5\%$
- Yellow–Violet–Orange–Gold $= 47 \times 1000 = 47\ \text{k}\Omega \pm 5\%$
